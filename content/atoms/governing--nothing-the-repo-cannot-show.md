# Nothing is asserted that the repo cannot show

The course skeleton's opening states the evidence rule turned on the course itself: every module
draws its evidence from journey chapters, so a module with no artifact behind it cannot be taught.
Three of the seven modules started that way — opinion only — and they are what the remaining
chapters exist to supply.

The test of it is module 5, which asks the learner to distinguish a check that passes from a check
that *could have failed* — and to demand the demonstration. That is the one habit from this whole
journey that transfers to any job, with or without an agent.
