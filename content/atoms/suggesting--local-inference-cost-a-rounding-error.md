# A whole era of local completions was a rounding error

The cost question at the suggesting rung is not the sticker price of a tool but
what a whole era of use comes to once it is measured on the same scale as the
rest. Keeping inference on the home box answers it: the marginal money is a
rounding error because the tokens were generated locally, not billed per call.

The figure below is this era's slice of the combined token floor across every
tool that left a record. It is the smallest of the four by three orders of
magnitude — the shape of what self-hosting bought.
