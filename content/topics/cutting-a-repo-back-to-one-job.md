# Cutting a repo back to one job

A repository rarely sets out to do more than one thing. It acquires the second
job because a piece of scaffolding turned out to be reusable, and the third
because the tooling around the first two became interesting in its own right.
None of the individual additions look wrong; the accumulation does.

The material grouped here is about noticing that from outside the code, and
about the form the answer takes. Asking what to delete produces an argument.
Asking for a ranked plan, with a fixed number of items and a sizing on each,
produces a list you can rule on one row at a time.
