# RAG simplify & align

A run of source threads on `kri-local-rag`, named after their own filename
prefix in the corpus. What they share is not the subject — retrieval, CI, Docker
layout, the EU AI Act — but the shape of the ask: the whole repository goes in,
and what comes back is a numbered list of proposals to rule on rather than a
patch to apply.

That shape is what puts these threads at the planning rung. The atoms below are
mined from them.
